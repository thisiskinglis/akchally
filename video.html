Section 05 — Not just editing

The timeline isn't the thinking.

A good edit starts before the first cut. Video isn't simply: put clips together + add music + export.

Before editing, we need to understand:

  • Who is watching?
  • Where will they see it?
  • What should they understand?
  • What should they remember?
  • What should happen afterward?

Then we decide what the video actually needs.

Because 47 beautiful clips arranged randomly are still 47 beautiful clips arranged randomly.

Section 07 — Your phone

Your phone isn't necessarily the problem.

Bad footage and imperfect footage aren't the same thing. Modern phones can capture perfectly usable material for many business applications.

What matters is how it's captured.

If we're developing a video from an idea, we can give you practical guidance on:

  • What to film
  • Orientation
  • Framing
  • Lighting
  • Audio considerations
  • Clip length
  • Backgrounds
  • Additional detail shots
  • How to send the files

So you don't discover after filming everything that none of it can actually be used.

Section 08 — What remote means

A useful distinction.

We produce remotely.

AKCHALLY does not currently position itself as an on-location film crew.

That means we are not flying internationally with cameras to film your office.

You or your local team capture the required footage — or provide existing assets — and we handle the agreed creative and production work remotely.

If a project genuinely requires specialist local filming, we'll tell you.

We would rather define the boundary properly than pretend otherwise.

Section 14 — FAQ

Before you send 14GB of footage.

Can you work with phone footage?

Yes, depending on quality and intended use. If you're filming specifically for the project, we'll provide practical capture guidance where included in scope.

Do you film the footage?

AKCHALLY currently works remotely. Clients provide existing footage or capture footage locally according to the agreed project requirements.

Can you help with the script?

Yes. Basic scripting and content structure can be included depending on the project.

Can you source music?

Where required, licensed music can be incorporated subject to the agreed scope and any third-party licensing costs.

Can you make different versions for Instagram, LinkedIn and the website?

Yes. Additional cuts and formats can be included or quoted separately.

How many revisions are included?

Two consolidated revision rounds are standard unless your quote states otherwise.

Can you fix terrible footage?

Sometimes. We can improve and work around many imperfections. We cannot recreate information that wasn't captured. If the source material genuinely isn't usable, we'll tell you.
