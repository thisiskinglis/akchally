Why This Exists

Because "we need a new website" isn't always the answer.

Sometimes the website is fine. Sometimes the real issue is the company profile. Or the messaging. Or the imagery. Or the Google presence. Or the fact that every customer-facing asset has been made separately over five years.

If you replace the wrong thing, you've spent money without fixing the actual problem.

The Look-Over exists to identify what actually needs changing first.
