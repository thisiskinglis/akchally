Section 03 — The Output — No 40-page strategy deck

KEEP.

What's already working.

The things we would leave alone. The parts of your presence that are doing their job.

FIX.

What's weakening the presentation.

Not necessarily broken. Just not helping. Dated copy, bad spacing, weak hierarchy, inconsistent imagery.

INCONSISTENT.

Where the business stops looking like one coherent business.

Different fonts. Different tone. Different colours. Different messaging. Different levels of quality.

MISSING.

Things customers or stakeholders should be able to find or understand but currently can't.

The priority order.

Because not everything needs fixing at once. We tell you what we would address first, second and later.

We're not doing:

  • Full marketing audits
  • Operational diagnosis

The Look-Over is much narrower. It looks at how the business currently presents itself externally and whether that presentation feels clear, credible and coherent. That's it.

Section 12 — FAQ

Before we look.

How much material can I send?

Enough for us to understand the core external presence. If the business has an unusually large number of brands, websites or materials, we'll confirm whether the standard Look-Over scope is appropriate before beginning.

Is this a full brand audit?

No. It is a Business Presence & Cohesion Review, not a full strategic brand audit.

Will you review our competitors?

Not as standard. The focus is your current external presence. Competitive analysis can be scoped separately if genuinely useful.

Will you redesign anything during the Look-Over?

No. The Look-Over identifies and prioritises. Design and production are separate projects.

Do I have to hire AKCHALLY afterward?

No.

What if everything is actually fine?

Then we'll tell you. A review that concludes “don't spend money changing this” is still useful.

How quickly do I receive it?

Your confirmed turnaround is provided before the project begins.

Can the $250 be used toward other AKCHALLY work?

It may be credited toward a qualifying Do-Over commenced within 30 days.
