Section 02 — Not just pretty pages

We don't start by asking what colour you want the buttons.

A website has several jobs.

It needs to explain the business.
It needs to establish credibility.
It needs to help people find what they're looking for.
It needs to work properly on a phone.
And somewhere in all of that, it needs to make somebody do something.

So we don't start by asking what colour you want the buttons.

We start with:

  • What is this business?
  • Who needs to understand it?
  • What do they need to know?
  • What should happen next?

Then we build around that.

Section 06 — Important SEO Distinction

We build the foundations. We don't sell fairy dust.


Every AKCHALLY website is structured with foundational on-page SEO in mind. That means we're helping search engines understand what the site is, what its pages are about and how they should be indexed.

It does not mean:

  • Guaranteed Google rankings
  • Ongoing content production
  • Backlink campaigns
  • Paid search management
  • Monthly SEO retainers
  • Promises to make you “#1 on Google”

Those are different services. If you need an ongoing SEO campaign, we'll tell you that. If you don't, we won't invent one.

Section 12 — FAQ

The questions people usually ask.

How long does a website take?

It depends on size and complexity. Your quote will include an expected project timeline before we begin.

Do I need to provide all the copy?

No. We can work from existing business material and refine it into web copy. Substantial from-scratch copywriting may be quoted separately.

Can you use my existing domain?

Yes. If you already own your domain, we'll connect the new website to it.

Do I own the website?

Once the project is fully paid, the agreed final website assets and client-owned accounts are yours, subject to any third-party platform or licence terms.

Can you maintain it afterward?

Small post-launch changes can be quoted as required. AKCHALLY does not force clients into a monthly maintenance retainer.

Will it work on mobile?

Yes. Responsive design is standard.

Is SEO included?

Foundational on-page SEO and search setup are included. Ongoing SEO campaigns are not.

How many changes can I make?

Standard website projects include two consolidated revision rounds unless your quote states otherwise.
