Section 04 — The Real Problem

It's not always ugly.

Sometimes the document simply doesn't know what it's trying to say.

A beautiful document with bad information architecture is still a bad document.

So before we worry about colours and photographs, we look at:

  • What is this document for?
  • Who will read it?
  • What do they need to understand?
  • What matters most?
  • What's repeated?
  • What's missing?
  • What can disappear entirely?

Then we design.

Making a confusing document prettier just gives you a beautiful confusing document.

Structure before decoration.

Section 11 — What we don't do

Useful boundaries.

We're not a typing service. AKCHALLY is a good fit when the document needs thinking and making.

We're probably not the right fit if you simply need:

  • Bulk data entry
  • Routine word processing
  • Hundreds of pages reformatted mechanically
  • Academic assignments
  • Legal drafting
  • Technical content requiring specialist subject-matter authorship
  • Someone available indefinitely to make tiny edits

And if you need us to create claims, figures or facts that the business cannot substantiate: also no.

We make the information work. We don't manufacture the information.

Section 13 — FAQ

Before you send the monstrosity.

Can you rewrite the content?

Yes. Copy refinement and restructuring can form part of the project. If substantial from-scratch writing is required, we'll define that in the quote.

Do I need professional photographs?

Not necessarily. We'll assess the assets you have and tell you if additional imagery is actually required.

Can you follow our existing brand guidelines?

Absolutely. If you have established brand guidelines, we'll work within them.

What if we don't have brand guidelines?

We can work from the existing visual identity or recommend a Tidy-Up if the inconsistencies are substantial enough to affect the project.

Can you make it print-ready?

Yes, where print production is part of the agreed scope.

Will I receive an editable version?

That depends on the project and software used. Your quote will state the final file formats and whether editable source files are included.

How many revisions are included?

Two consolidated revision rounds are standard unless otherwise stated.

Can you work with confidential business material?

Yes. Confidentiality requirements can be agreed before material is supplied.
