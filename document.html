It's Not Always Ugly.

Sometimes the document simply doesn't know what it's trying to say.

A beautiful document with bad information architecture is still a bad document.

Before we design, we work out:

  • → What is this for?
  • → Who will read it?
  • → What do they need to understand?
  • → What matters most?
  • → What's repeated?
  • → What's missing?
  • → What can disappear entirely?

Structure before decoration.

Because making a confusing document prettier just gives you a beautiful confusing document.
